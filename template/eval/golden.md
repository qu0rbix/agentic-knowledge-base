# Golden questions (regression suite)

Format: `Q01 | question | expected answer if known | category | notes: <note-id>,<note-id>`
- `notes:` lists the notes that answer it (the librarian fills it in once filed). `notes:` left empty = the KB should NOT answer this (out of scope / not tracked) - keep at least 20 of those; they test "not in KB" and calibrate semantic search.
