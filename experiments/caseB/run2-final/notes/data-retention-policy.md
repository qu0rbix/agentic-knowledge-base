## Claims
- Customer personal data in the Orion warehouse is retained for 24 months after the customer's last order and is then purged by the monthly purge job (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 2.1 "Customer personal data in the Orion warehouse is retained for 24 months after the customer's last order and is then purged by the monthly purge job."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Raw clickstream events are retained for 13 months; aggregated clickstream tables may be kept longer only if they contain no personal data (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 2.2 "Raw clickstream events are retained for 13 months."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 2.2 "Aggregated clickstream tables may be kept longer if they contain no personal data."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Application logs are kept for 90 days in the logging platform, which is not a system of record (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 2.3 "Application logs are kept for 90 days in the logging platform."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 2.3 "Logs are not a system of record and should not be queried for reporting."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Any exception to the policy requires written sign-off from the Head of Data, Helen Okafor; exceptions are logged in the policy register and reviewed each quarter. [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Document owner: Helen Okafor, Head of Data"] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 4.1 "Exceptions to any rule in this policy require written sign-off from the Head of Data."]

- Where this policy and the register disagree, the register wins and the discrepancy should be reported to the policy owner (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 1. Purpose and scope "Where this policy and the register disagree, the register wins and the discrepancy should be reported to the policy owner."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
