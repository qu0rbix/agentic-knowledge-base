## Claims
- Access to production tables containing PII requires written approval from the data owner and from Security (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 3.1 "Access to production tables containing PII requires written approval from the data owner and from Security."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Temporary PII access is granted for no more than 90 days, expires automatically, and can be renewed once (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 3.2 "Temporary PII access is granted for no more than 90 days and expires automatically; it can be renewed once."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Production PII extracts must not be stored on shared drives or in personal cloud storage; the secure analysis workspace must be used instead (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 3.3 "Production PII extracts must not be stored on shared drives or in personal cloud storage. Use the secure analysis workspace instead."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
- Emergency access during an incident may be granted by the on-call lead and must be reported to Security within one business day (policy v3.1, effective 1 September 2026). [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy 4.2 "Emergency access during an incident may be granted by the on-call lead and must be reported to Security within one business day."] [src: 2026-09-25-data-retention-policy header "Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026, supersedes version 3.0"]
